Creating Sustainable Impact

6 KEY INITIATIVES OF INAMIGOS

Hunger Relief

Project Sewa

Serving cooked meals, dry grocery kits, and warm clothing to daily wage workers, street dwellers, and disaster-affected families across Chhattisgarh and other states.

Over 50,000+ meals distributed to date.
Food & Clothing
Child Education

Project Bachpanshala

Providing free evening classes, school bags, notebooks, stationery, and digital education workshops for children who lack access to formal schooling.

2,500+ children enrolled across centers.
Free Education
Animal Welfare

Project Jeev

Dedicated to animal protection, emergency rescue for injured stray dogs and cows, anti-rabies vaccination drives, and establishing daily feeding spots.

1,200+ street animals fed and treated weekly.
Animal Care
Women Empowerment

Project Udaan

Fostering financial independence through sewing/tailoring skill centers, computer literacy workshops, and distributing eco-friendly sanitary products.

800+ women trained for self-employment.
Skill Training
Environment

Project Prakriti

Mass tree plantation drives, water conservation campaigns, distribution of saplings, and plastic-free community cleanliness movements.

10,000+ trees planted and maintained.
Plantation
Youth Employability

Project Vikas

Offering youth skill enhancement workshops, resume building, communication training, and hands-on non-profit internships (#InAmigos).

1,500+ youth interned & trained.
Youth Skills

InAmigos Foundation

A Section 8 registered non-profit organization dedicated to food security, child education, animal welfare, women empowerment, environmental protection, and skill development across India.
