Our Journey & Philosophy

Serving Humanity with Compassion & Integrity

InAmigos Foundation was established on September 23, 2020, under the visionary leadership of Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). We are a Section 8 registered non-profit organization licensed directly by the Central Government of India, headquartered in Chhattisgarh.

What started as a small group of passionate youth responding to food insecurity has expanded into a multi-state humanitarian initiative reaching across 28 Indian states with a dedicated family of over 200 volunteers.

At InAmigos, we firmly believe in the transformative power of collective action. Every initiative—whether feeding homeless citizens, educating underprivileged children, aiding stray animals, or empowering women—is executed with high operational excellence and transparency.

Our Purpose

MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To uplift vulnerable and underprivileged sections of society by providing immediate relief, sustainable quality education, healthcare, animal welfare, and skill development programs that create long-term self-reliance.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive, compassionate, and empowered nation where no child is deprived of education, no family suffers from hunger, no animal faces cruelty, and women enjoy full socio-economic equality.
