Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are donations to InAmigos Foundation tax deductible?

Yes! InAmigos Foundation holds valid 80G and 12A certifications under the Income Tax Act. Donors receive official receipts to claim 50% tax exemption.

How can I apply for an internship or volunteer role?

You can apply directly on our Volunteers page. We offer virtual and on-field roles in content writing, graphic design, project coordination, and field drives.

Where is InAmigos Foundation headquartered?

We are headquartered in Chhattisgarh, India, with active volunteer networks and projects expanding across 28 states nationwide.

InAmigos Foundation

A Section 8 registered non-profit organization dedicated to food security, child education, animal welfare, women empowerment, environmental protection, and skill development across India.
