About InAmigos Foundation

Get to Know Us Better

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization licensed by the Central Government, based in Chhattisgarh, India.

It holds 80G & 12A certifications, ensuring complete transparency, accountability, and tax-exempt benefits for all donors. Our foundation is also CSR-1 registered to partner with corporations, NITI Aayog registered, and holds the prestigious IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification for quality operations.
